**Best options**  
- **Air India** or **Air France** from Delhi/Mumbai give the cheapest direct fares.  
- A one‑stop flight via **Lufthansa (Frankfurt)** or **Emirates (Dubai)** can sometimes be 5–10 % cheaper.

**Booking tips**  
1. **Book 2–3 months ahead** – fares usually spike 6–8 weeks before departure.  
2. Use **Google Flights / Skyscanner** with “flexible dates” to see the cheapest day of the week.  
3. Set price alerts for your chosen route.  
4. If you’re flying from a city with no direct, consider a “hub‑hop” – e.g. **Bengaluru → Delhi → CDG** – it can cut ₹10 000–₹15 000.  
5. Check baggage allowances; a budget carrier may add ₹5 000–₹8 000 per person for checked luggage.
