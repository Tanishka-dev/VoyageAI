### 1️⃣  Pre‑Trip Checklist  

| Item | Cost (₹) | Notes |
|------|----------|-------|
| **Round‑trip flight** (Delhi/Mumbai → Tokyo) | 30 000 – 55 000 | Book 2–3 months ahead. Use one‑stop via Seoul/Dubai/Doha for cheapest fares. |
| **Japan Tourist Visa** | 3 200 – 3 800 | Apply at VFS or online (if eligible). 30‑day single entry. |
| **JR 7‑Day Pass** | ≈20 000 (≈¥29 000) | Covers Shinkansen & local JR lines. Buy at airport or online. |
| **Accommodation** | 32 000 – 40 000 | 8 nights in hostels/capsule hotels (₹3 000–5 000/night). |
| **Local transport (subways, buses, Suica)** | 5 000 | Add a Suica/ICOCA card (¥2 000). |
| **Food** | 15 000 | 3 meals/day at konbini / ramen stalls (₹300–₹600/meal). |
| **Sight‑seeing & entry fees** | 5 000 | Free shrines, museums (some have free days). |
| **Contingency / souvenirs** | 5 000 | Extra buffer. |
| **Total** | **≈1,300,000** | ~₹1.3 lakh (≈¥1 200 000). |

> **Tip:** Book flights + hotels together on sites like Expedia, Agoda, or directly with airlines (Air India, JAL) to get small discounts.

### 2️⃣  Suggested Travel Window  
**15 – 23 June** (or any non‑peak week).  
- Avoid cherry‑blossom (Mar‑Apr) and autumn‑foliage (Oct‑Nov) windows.  
- June gives mild weather (23–25 °C) and lower prices.

> **Weather Forecast (Oct 1):** 22–23 °C, light rain, 90 % humidity.  
> Pack a lightweight, waterproof jacket and an umbrella.
