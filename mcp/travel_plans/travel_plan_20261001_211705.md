2. **Use public transport** – metro, trams, and buses. Buy daily/weekly passes.
3. **Book *regional* trains early** – cheaper than high‑speed (Frecciarossa/Italo).  
4. **Free attractions** – many churches, piazzas, and museums have free entry on certain days.
5. **Travel light** – avoid checked‑in baggage fees on low‑cost airlines.
6. **Book multi‑city tickets** – e.g., Delhi→Rome, Rome→Milan, Milan→Delhi – often cheaper than separate round‑trips.
7. **Set price alerts** – Skyscanner, Google Flights, Momondo.
